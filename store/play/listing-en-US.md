## Short description (max 80)

## Full description (max 4000)

Parkour·Spot is a community map of parkour spots around the world. Find somewhere to train near you, see what a spot is like before you go, and put the places you love on the map for everyone else.

Built by and for the parkour community. Exploring the map is free and open, with no account needed.

FIND SPOTS NEAR YOU
• Browse thousands of spots on the map, from local walls and rails to famous training grounds
• Search any city or spot by name, or jump straight to where you are
• The best-rated spots show first, so you see the good stuff right away

SEE THE SPOT BEFORE YOU GO
• Photos from people who train there
• Community ratings
• What a spot is good for: vaults, precisions, flow, flips, strides and more
• Features, access (public, restricted or paid) and facilities like cover, lighting, water and toilets

FILTER FOR WHAT YOU NEED
• Rainy day? Show covered spots only
• Filter by access, facilities, photos and spot sources

NEVER MISS A JAM
• Jams, workshops, classes and comps on the same map as the spots
• See what's happening now and what's coming up near you
• Follow what the community has added recently

PLAN YOUR TRAINING
• Save spots you want to visit and mark the ones you've been to
• Make lists for a city trip, a jam route or your crew
• Check in at a spot and plan sessions so others can join

SHARE WITH THE COMMUNITY
• Add a new spot with photos. It's on the map right away
• Propose an event. Moderators review it before it goes live
• Rate spots and help keep the map accurate
• Share any spot or list with a link

Parkour·Spot is available in English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish and Portuguese. You can also use it in your browser at parkour.spot.

Missing a spot in your city? Add it. The map is only as good as the community that builds it.
